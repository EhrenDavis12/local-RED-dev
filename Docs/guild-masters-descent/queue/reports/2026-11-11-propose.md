# Proposal: first build plan toward the Unity MVP

> **Status:** Mastermind proposal of 2026-11-11, from forge-queue-planner against the Goal in
> `queue/focus.md`. Queued as-is under the user's standing yes.

Nothing is built yet: `src/guildmasters-descent` has one initial commit. The whole MVP is the
gap. 23 rows in build order; eight are `decide:` rows that park until the user answers.

## Proposed

PROPOSAL: Unity project scaffold: iOS-targeted Unity 6000.6.2f1 project with a pure-C# game-core assembly, EditMode tests, the one placeholder tuning file and a labelled bright-yellow placeholder shape [look] [S] — the srcRoot is empty and every other row builds inside it.
PROPOSAL: decide: where a member's health comes from, whether damage carries from battle to battle within a dive, whether falling in a dungeon battle is death, and what health a member has back in the hall [research] [S] — no doc defines health, yet combat, the Cook, rest-area healing and permanent death all turn on it; blocks both combat rows and the dive.
PROPOSAL: decide: what the dungeon's enemies are — whether they use the six combat types with their attacks and specials, whether they carry gear, and where their stats come from [research] [S] — the docs give enemies a grid, targeting and repositioning but no roster or stats ("nine Brutes" is the only hint, and the board speaks of "monster and enemy"); blocks the combat engine and the dive.
PROPOSAL: decide: what a dungeon floor is beyond its 2–3 battles — the "floor layout" the board reveals, whether the party picks a route, and what makes a boss floor [research] [S] — without it a dive can only be built as a plain list of battles; blocks the dive.
PROPOSAL: decide: what loot is and how it is carried — which items and materials exist at F rank and what an item does, when loot drops, how carrying capacity is measured and what happens when it is full, what retreating loses and whether a party can leave anywhere but a rest area, and what class dropped gear rolls at [research] [S] — "retreating always costs the loot gathered since the last safe floor" next to "how much is lost depends on who came along" does not say what the floor-5 choice keeps; blocks the dive, the Porter, the shop's sell side and repairing broken gear.
PROPOSAL: decide: how a mission's outcome is decided — what makes it succeed or fail, which members are injured on a failure, and what the "backend variance" moves [research] [S] — the docs give what failure costs but no way to reach it; blocks the missions row.
PROPOSAL: decide: how a new F-rank guild gets its first dungeon — does it start with one, or must it find one on a mission [research] [S] — the docs reveal dungeons only by ranking up (not included) or by a small chance on a mission, so a new game may have nothing to dive; blocks new-game setup and the dungeon board.
PROPOSAL: decide: portrait or landscape on the iPhone [research] [S] — the docs say phone but not which way up, and every screen's layout follows from it; blocks the screen rows.
PROPOSAL: Guild and member data model: gold, stores, roster cap, members (type, rank or tier, level, XP, five stats, injury), gear pieces with rolled range and durability, starting stats from type and rank, weighted level-up, the first promotion at level 16 for gold [prd] [M] — the save file and every screen read this shape, so a wrong guess is a save migration.
PROPOSAL: Save, load and time passing while closed: mission timers, injury healing and recruiting-board restock settled from the wall clock on load [prd] [M] — persistence and offline progress, so a wrong guess loses or corrupts saves; needs the data model.
PROPOSAL: Combat engine: Dexterity turn order, damage and defence from stat plus gear, per-strike hit and defence rolls, Luck evasion, area attacks that cannot miss and pay reduced defence, gear wear and breaking mid-sequence, the support ring with diminishing help, the type-advantage multiplier, enemy targeting with taunt, special bars with base charge [prd] [L] — every battle runs on these rules, so a wrong guess means rewriting everything built on top; needs the health and enemy decisions.
PROPOSAL: The six combat types' attacks, specials and bonus charge conditions (cleave, Shields Up, flurry, Vanish, Volley and its streak, column attack and lingering field), plus the specialists' weak close attack and missing special bar [prd] [L] — each type's shape is what the matchups are balanced on, so a wrong guess breaks the triangles; builds on the engine.
PROPOSAL: Copy the approved character art in from the animation checkout, only what annie marks done, and list every missing asset for the animation side [look] [S] — six characters (knight, archer, barbarian, healer, mage, rogue: idle, attack, defend, hurt, skill, burst) are the only art; everything else is a yellow shape.
PROPOSAL: Battle screen: both 5×3 grids, highlighted turn, auto-selected default attack, party-wide focus target, special button when the bar is full, hits and deaths shown [look] [L] — the core of how the game feels, checked by playing it; needs both combat rows and the art.
PROPOSAL: Title menu, new game, load, settings, and the guild hall with its three boards as entry points [look] [M] — the shell every hall screen hangs from; needs the orientation and first-dungeon decisions and save/load.
PROPOSAL: Recruiting board: F-rank divers plus the Cook and the Porter, type and rank shown plainly with a rough impression and no numbers, gold to hire, restock over time, roster cap, a chance to arrive carrying gear [look] [M] — how the player gets a first party; the docs settle the rules and prices are placeholders.
PROPOSAL: decide: "Does the player see a piece's exact rolled range and durability in the Dorms, or only a rough impression the way the recruiting board shows a candidate's strengths?" [research] [S] — an open question about what the Dorms shows on every gear piece.
PROPOSAL: Dorms and roster: each member's stats, level, XP, rank, gear and injury; assign gear from the stores; pay to promote at the threshold; pay to speed healing; dismiss; repair gear in the hall [look] [M] — the character-management half of the Goal; needs the data model and the gear-display decision.
PROPOSAL: Shop: buy F-baseline weapons and armour rolled from their bands, sell items and materials [look] [M] — the sell-and-re-equip step of the loop; the sell side needs the loot decision.
PROPOSAL: Mission board and timed missions: a few F-rank missions, the F slot cap, members away and unavailable while out, timers that run while closed, gold and item rewards, injury on failure [prd] [M] — timers live in the save and outcomes are rules, so a wrong guess is a save migration; needs the mission-outcome decision.
PROPOSAL: The dive: one dungeon at least 10 floors deep, 2–3 battles a floor, rest areas at 5 and 10 with healing and the push-on-or-go-home choice, an entry fee that rises with party size, re-entry at the last rest area for the entry fee plus a teleport fee, XP per floor, permanent death, the dead's gear brought back if there is room, the Cook's healing and the Porter's capacity [prd] [L] — the main loop and what it writes to the save, so a wrong guess loses members or loot the player earned; needs the health, enemy, floor and loot decisions.
PROPOSAL: Dungeon board: dungeon information revealed floor by floor, deepest floor reached against depth, party planning (pick members, then a zoomed grid with auto-placement that puts combat types in front and makes support links), enter and re-enter [look] [M] — the way into the dive; needs the dive and the battle grid.
PROPOSAL: decide: "What does changing formation mid-battle cost the player, if anything?" [research] [S] — the docs make mid-battle formation changes part of combat but leave the cost open; it blocks only that feature, so the battle screen ships without it until then.

## Treated as numbers, not rules

The type-advantage multiplier, how fast a special charges, how long effects last, XP per level,
whether XP grows with depth, the armour gain per quality step, the weapon-kind damage order,
rolled-span widths, all prices and fees, health pool size, and injury duration. Under the Goal
they become placeholders in the tuning file instead of blocking. If any of them is a rule, it
needs its own `decide:` row.

## Needs your call

   MVP? The Goal names the three boards but not training. If it is in, it needs two answers
   first: the open question "How far can a training mission level a member?", and how a
   training run's outcome is decided.
2. Which approved character plays which type? Knight can cover Tank or Fighter but not both,
   and there is no Healer type. Should the healer art stand in for the Cook, or go unused?
