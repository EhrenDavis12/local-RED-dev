## Change: Every member starts with 10 health

> Proposed 2026-11-11 · for review

**Where:** `## Starting Stats`, second paragraph (a new paragraph after it)

**Now says:**

```
A newly recruited level-1 F-rank member starts with single-digit stats.
```

**Proposed:**

```
A newly recruited level-1 F-rank member starts with single-digit stats.

Every member starts with 10 health. For now that's a basic number, and a placeholder to
be tuned.
```

**Why:** Answered: let's just start with a basic health number at 10. No doc said what
health a member has, though The Dungeon's rest area "where the party heals" and Combat's
enemy targeting on "how easy it is to kill" both rely on one. The existing paragraph is
unchanged. Not settled by this: whether health grows with level, rank or tier; whether
Constitution plays into it; whether damage carries from one battle to the next within a
dive; whether reaching 0 health in a dungeon battle is death; and what health a member has
back in the hall. For the owner to reconcile: the first paragraph says a C-rank hire
"starts higher across the board than an F-rank one", and health is the same 10 for every
member — that holds only while health isn't counted among the stats rank scales.
