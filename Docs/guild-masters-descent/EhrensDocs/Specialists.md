# Specialists — proposed changes

> **Status:** Change notes against `Specialists.md`, for its owner to review. The original
> is not edited.

## Change: Two Cooks heal twice as much, a dead Cook adds nothing, and a Cook heals the same at every level, for now

> Proposed 2026-11-13 · for review

**Where:** `## The Cook`

**Now says:**

```
The Cook heals a little between floors — another five per cent — and substantially more at
a rest area — a further fifteen per cent — on top of what the party gets anyway.
```

**Proposed:**

```
The Cook heals a little between floors — another five per cent — and substantially more at
a rest area — a further fifteen per cent — on top of what the party gets anyway.
For now two Cooks heal twice as much, a Cook who has died before the heal adds nothing,
and each living Cook's share goes to every living member, the Cook included. For now,
too, a Cook's five and fifteen per cent are the same at every level and every tier.
```

**Why:** The dive is built this way. Nothing says what a second Cook adds, or whether a
Cook who dies on the floor still heals. The flat share pulls against `## Tiers`, "For a
specialist, promotion makes the same ability stronger", and Guild Members.md's "their
specialty improves alongside" — neither gives a rule for the Cook, so the dive heals the
same at every level until one is settled. What a second Cook adds and how a Cook's heal
grows are proposed as questions for `## Open Questions`.
