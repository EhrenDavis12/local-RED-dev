## Change: Whom the Cook heals, for now

> Proposed 2026-11-12 · for review

**Where:** `## The Cook`

**Now says:**

```
The Cook heals a little after every battle, and substantially more at a rest area — on top
of the rest area's own healing.
```

**Proposed:**

```
The Cook heals a little after every battle, and substantially more at a rest area — on top
of the rest area's own healing. For now a Cook heals every living member of the party, the
Cook included, and two Cooks heal twice as much. How much a Cook heals is a placeholder,
to be balanced later.
```

**Why:** The dive is built this way. Nothing said whom the Cook heals, or what a second
Cook adds; that's proposed as a question for `## Open Questions`.
