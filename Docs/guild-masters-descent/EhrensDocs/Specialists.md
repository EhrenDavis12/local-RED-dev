## Change: Two Cooks heal twice as much, for now

> Proposed 2026-11-12 · for review

**Where:** `## The Cook`

**Now says:**

```
The Cook heals a little between floors — another five per cent — and substantially more at
a rest area — a further fifteen per cent — on top of what the party gets anyway.
```

```
The Cook heals a little between floors — another five per cent — and substantially more at
a rest area — a further fifteen per cent — on top of what the party gets anyway.
For now two Cooks heal twice as much.
```

**Why:** The dive is built this way. Nothing says what a second Cook adds; that's proposed
as a question for `## Open Questions`.
